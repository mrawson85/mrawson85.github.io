QUESTION
What do FSH, LH, and testosterone do in males?
ANSWER
FSH → sperm cell production. LH → testosterone production. Testosterone → sperm cells + secondary sex chars. Testosterone inhibits FSH and LH (negative feedback).
💡 Memory tip: 🔄 "F makes Sperm, L makes T, T makes everything" — FSH=Sperm, LH=Testosterone, T=both!

🔢 Umbilical Cord: "AAV" / "2-1 Rule"
2 Arteries + 1 Vein = AAV. Think of it as a road: 2 lanes going TO the placenta (arteries), 1 lane coming BACK (vein).
The navelstreng has two slagaders carrying waste TO the placenta, one ader bringing nutrients BACK.
🔢 1-2-3: Born-Body-Brain
Primary (1°) = Born with it. Secondary (2°) = Body changes in puberty. Tertiary (3°) = Brain/mental changes.
Sex characteristics come in 3 levels, easy to remember with the 3 B's.
📢 Hormone Chain: "FELiPro"
FSH → Estrogen → LH → Progesterone. Say "FE-Li-Pro" like a superhero name! FSH starts follicles, Estrogen grows things, LH triggers ovulation, Progesterone protects the lining.
This is the female menstrual cycle hormone cascade in order.
🟡 Yellow Light = Corpus Luteum
Yellow body = yellow traffic light = CAUTION, keep things stable! Progesterone holds the lining. When it dies → RED light = menstruation (bleeding). HCG = green light to KEEP the yellow light on during pregnancy.
Traffic light metaphor: Yellow=stable, Red=menstruation, Green(HCG)=pregnancy continues.
🤰 HCG = Help Corpus Going!
HCG is made by the developing placenta. Its job: SAVE the corpus luteum so progesterone stays high → no menstruation → pregnancy continues!
This is why pregnancy tests detect HCG — it's only there during pregnancy!
✂️ Meiosis = ME dividing TWICE
1 diploid (2n) → divide → divide → 4 haploid (n). Two cuts! Diploid = Double (pairs). Haploid = Half (singles).
Key: diploid (2n) has chromosome PAIRS, haploid (n) has SINGLES.
🔋 ATP vs ADP = Battery Charge
ATP = 3 phosphates = FULL battery 🔋🔋🔋. ADP = 2 phosphates = DRAINED battery 🔋🔋. Breaking off 1 phosphate releases energy!
Tri=3, Di=2. Energy is stored in the bond of the 3rd phosphate group.
💨 AERO = AIR, AN-aero = NO AIR
Aerobic dissimilation = WITH oxygen → lots of ATP (full breakdown). Anaerobic = WITHOUT oxygen → little ATP. Alcoholic fermentation (anaerobic): glucose → ethanol + CO₂ → only 2 ATP!
Think of an aerobics class: you breathe hard (use O₂). Without air = fermentation.
🌳 Phloem DOWN, Xylem UP
Phloem = Food from leaves going DOWN. Xylem = X-tra water from roots going UP. Mnemonic: "Phloem = Phlow down" and "Xylem = X-cend (ascend)"
Two transport systems in plants: one for sugar (down), one for water+minerals (up).
🎬 Birth Phases: D.E.A. / O.U.N.
Dilation → Expulsion → Afterbirth. Or in Dutch: Ontsluiting → Uitdrijving → Nageboorte. Oxytocin is the hormone that drives contractions.
3 clear steps. The afterbirth (placenta) comes last!
⛓️ Limiting Factor = Weakest Link
A chain breaks at its weakest link. In biology: the factor FURTHEST from its optimum controls the reaction speed. Fix the worst factor first!
Example: if light is abundant but CO₂ is low, CO₂ is the limiting factor for photosynthesis.
📸 Photosynthesis = Photo + Synthesis
PHOTO = light, SYNTHESIS = building. Plants BUILD glucose USING light. Ingredients: CO₂ + H₂O + light → glucose + O₂. Stomata (huidmondjes) let CO₂ in.
The reverse of dissimilation! Plants capture light energy and store it as glucose.

🧬 Embryonic Development
Fertilization happens in the oviduct: sperm nucleus + egg nucleus → zygote Binas 86E
Zygote divides via cleavage divisions → cilia transport it to uterine lining Binas 86E
Blastula = hollow ball of cells (early embryo stage)
Umbilical cord: 2 arteries + 1 vein → placenta exchanges substances Binas 84B, 86E
Amniotic fluid + membranes protect the fetus Binas 86E
Sex characteristics: Primary (birth) → Secondary (puberty, physical) → Tertiary (mental) Binas 86A, B
🔬 Gamete Formation
Meiosis: 1 diploid (2n) cell → 4 haploid (n) cells (two divisions) Binas 86
Semen = sperm cells + fluid from seminal vesicles + prostate Binas 86A2
Dizygotic twins: 2 eggs × 2 sperm. Monozygotic: 1 egg × 1 sperm → splits
⚗️ Hormonal Regulation (Female)
Pituitary starts puberty with FSH + LH Binas 86C, 89A/C
FSH → stimulates follicle ripening → follicles produce estrogens
Estrogens → secondary sex chars + uterine lining growth + inhibit FSH + stimulate LH → ovulation
Corpus luteum → progesterone → lining growth + inhibits FSH/LH
No fertilization → corpus luteum dies → progesterone ↓ → menstruation
Pregnancy: HCG saves corpus luteum → progesterone stays high → lining stays Binas 86E, 89A
⚗️ Hormonal Regulation (Male)
FSH → sperm cell formation. LH → testosterone production Binas 89A/C
Testosterone → sperm cells + secondary sex chars. Inhibits FSH and LH (negative feedback)
Leydig cells (interstitial cells between seminiferous tubules) produce testosterone
👶 Birth & Medical Help
3 phases: Dilation → Expulsion → Afterbirth. Hormone: oxytocin Binas 89A

Prenatal diagnostics or heel prick (hielprik) after birth to detect abnormalities
🛡️ STIs & Contraception
HIV medication suppresses the virus. Antibiotics treat bacterial STIs

⚡ Metabolism (Stofwisseling)
ATP (3 phosphates) = energy currency. ADP (2 phosphates) = spent form
Dissimilation = breakdown of organic molecules → energy. Aerobic (with O₂) vs Anaerobic (without O₂)
Alcoholic fermentation: glucose → ethanol + CO₂ (only 2 ATP)
Photosynthesis: CO₂ + H₂O + light → glucose + O₂ (in chloroplasts)
Stomata (huidmondjes) = ventilation openings surrounded by guard cells
Phloem (bast) = sugars DOWN from leaves. Xylem (hout) = water+minerals UP from roots
Root pressure (worteldruk) = osmotic force pushing water up through xylem
Limiting factor = factor furthest from optimum, controls reaction speed
